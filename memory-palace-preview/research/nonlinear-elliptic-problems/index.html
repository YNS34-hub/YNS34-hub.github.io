Nonlinear Elliptic Problems

Existence, growth and comparison.

My graduate research concerns nonlinear Dirichlet problems with general Orlicz-type growth and gradient-dependent lower-order terms. The current manuscript is in revision. The work includes proof development, verification, LaTeX preparation, literature review and reviewer-response revision. This exhibit summarizes an active research direction; it does not claim an accepted paper or a published theorem.

Research note

Equilibrium models often respond nonlinearly to their inputs. The central question is how to establish existence and useful comparison estimates when the growth law is more general than a fixed power and the right-hand side depends on the gradient. Rearrangement, symmetrization and Orlicz-space analysis provide the methods explored here.
