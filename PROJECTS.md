## Publishing standard for future projects

For future public repositories, I aim to keep a consistent minimum standard:

1. Clear one-sentence project purpose
2. Honest maturity label: `concept`, `prototype`, `active`, or `archived`
3. Explicit statement of my role when AI agents or external code are involved
4. Screenshots or a live demo when visual output matters
5. Reproducible local setup instructions
6. Tech stack listed without overstating proficiency
7. Clear distinction between original work and forks / references
8. Known limitations documented before future plans

This index will evolve as projects become validated, completed, or replaced by stronger work.
