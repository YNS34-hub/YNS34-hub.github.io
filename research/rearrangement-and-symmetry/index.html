Rearrangement & Symmetry

Transform the geometry. Preserve the information.

A spatial study of rearrangement and symmetrization, two methods used in my research. A function's level sets can be reorganized into a symmetric model while preserving their distribution. The resulting geometry offers a way to reason about estimates and compare nonlinear solutions. The sculpture is an illustrative visualization, not a proof of a new result.

Research note

How much can geometry change while the distribution of values stays fixed? This exhibit connects nested level sets to symmetric comparison problems and the role of rearrangement in nonlinear analysis.

|{x : u(x) > t}| = |{x : u*(x) > t}|
